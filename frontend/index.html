cryptography & network security

Your face is not a password.

You can't change it after a leak. So FaceVault never stores it in the clear: every face becomes 128 numbers in your browser, is sealed with envelope encryption, and is only ever decrypted in memory, for milliseconds.

    the attacker's view

    Steal the database.
    Get noise.

    No faces, no names, no emails. Each person has their own data key, wrapped by a versioned master key that lives in a separate, scrypt-locked keyring. Emails are looked up through an HMAC blind index.

    • ✓ Bit-flips break the GCM tag: record flagged TAMPERED
    • ✓ Swapping rows breaks the AAD binding
    • ✓ Editing the audit log breaks the HMAC chain
